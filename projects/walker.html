CRM & ERP · Internal platform

Overview

Walker Red Blue is the internal operations platform for Design2Occupancy, a green building and energy-efficiency consultancy. It connects sales, project delivery and accounts in one system.

I built it as a Full Stack Developer at Octodo Solutions, from the backend services and APIs through to integrations with the company's accounting and CRM tools.

  • RoleFull Stack Developer
  • CompanyOctodo Solutions
  • TimelineJun 2024 – May 2026
  • AccessAdmin panel, login required

What I built

  • 400+ REST APIs and gRPC services across CRM, projects, reporting and notifications.
  • Domain services: Project Dashboard, Feasibility, EnergyQC, RepGenX, Notification, Support & Communication.
  • Work order and payment management.
  • RBAC, ABAC and PBAC access control with JWT and OAuth 2.0 authentication.
  • Real-time notifications over WebSockets, plus background processing.
  • Query and index tuning on PostgreSQL and MS SQL Server.

Integrations

The platform connects to the tools the team already used, so data doesn't have to be entered twice:
