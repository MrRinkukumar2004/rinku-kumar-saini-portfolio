About

I like the parts of software most people never see: the services, schemas and failure paths that decide whether a payment actually goes through.

I've spent a little over two years building web applications and backend systems with Node.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, MongoDB and Redis. Most of that work has been microservices, authentication and wiring products into third-party providers.

At WrathCode Technology I work on AGCX, a regulated crypto exchange: wallets, transactions, fiat on/off-ramp, KYC and security. Before that, at Octodo Solutions, I built the whole digital platform for Design2Occupancy, from the public website and CMS to the CRM, work orders, payments and a Tally ERP integration. That came to more than 400 REST and gRPC APIs.

  • Backend & full stackNode.js, TypeScript, React
  • Microservices & DDDClear service boundaries, gRPC between them
  • DataPostgreSQL, MongoDB and Redis: schemas, indexes, caching
  • Auth & accessOAuth 2.0 / OIDC, Passkeys, RBAC / ABAC / PBAC
  • DeliveryDocker and Kubernetes
